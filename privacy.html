Locked

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Locked, an iPhone app made by Nord Apps (“we”), based in Norway. The short version: there's no account, what you enter stays on your iPhone, and we don't track you.

Who is responsible

Nord Apps, Norway, is the data controller. Contact: nordlabs.support@gmail.com.

No account, no sign-in

Locked has no accounts and no sign-in. We don't ask for your email address or phone number.

What stays on your device

What you enter or create in the app is stored only on your iPhone:

  • your first name (optional) and your answers in the setup (goal, what gets in your way, your hardest time of day);
  • your commitment, favourite quotes, streak and lock-in history;
  • your reminder times and other settings.

Part of this is kept in a shared storage area on your iPhone (an “App Group”) so the Locked widget can show today's quote and your streak. This data is not sent to us or to any server of ours. We don't run servers that receive your data.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. To confirm whether you have Locked Pro, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app (not linked to your name or email), your purchase history for Locked, and basic technical details needed to check a purchase (such as app version and device type). This is used only to provide the subscription. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Photos

If you save a quote card or wallpaper, the app asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It can't read or see your existing photos.

Ads and tracking

Locked shows no ads. This version of Locked does not track you, does not use your device's advertising identifier and does not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

If this changes in a future version

We may add measurement tools from Meta or TikTok in a later version to see which ads bring people to Locked. If we do, we will update this policy first, and iOS will ask for your permission before any tracking. You will be able to say no and still use everything.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Data on your iPhone stays until you delete it. Deleting the Locked app (and its widget) removes everything stored by the app on your device. Your subscription itself is kept by Apple; RevenueCat keeps purchase records as described in its policy. For any request about your data, email us at nordlabs.support@gmail.com.

Your rights (EEA and UK)

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. Because we don't hold your app data on our servers, most requests can be met by deleting the app; for anything else, contact us. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or your local supervisory authority.

California (CCPA)

We do not sell or share your personal information as defined by the CCPA, and we don't use it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Children

Locked is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top. Important changes will be made before the app changes how it uses data.
